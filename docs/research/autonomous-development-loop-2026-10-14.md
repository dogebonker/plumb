# Autonomous local development loop — 14 October 2026

After actual product/architecture/interface/slices approval, new interactive plans run implementation, mechanical checks, independent review, executor repairs and independent acceptance as one loop. Routine fixes of existing promises no longer reopen slices approval merely because original work was completed. Discovery questions and frozen promises remain.

## What changed

- The original approved slices document contains a narrow local repair mandate. A post-completion acceptance failure creates a new corrective slice with its own base/test/head and milestone. Original completed parents and approved document bytes remain unchanged.
- A shared read-only hook/CLI validates exact original scope and known criteria, strict JSON records, branch/commit provenance, failure/input bytes, packet equality, every commit including revert/merge paths, pending source edits and clean completion. A directory/flag/hash alone is not approval or independent quality evidence.
- Ordinary corrective scope is a subset of original approved source/test paths. New paths, changed FR/NFR/API/architecture, proof waivers and external/destructive/credential decisions retain real gates. Old plans and headless PR approvals remain compatible.
- Review findings go back to the existing implementation executor as one bounded batch. Reviewers stay read-only; the conductor coordinates and verifies. One general reviewer, risk specialists, isolated assertion RED/GREEN and independent acceptance remain.
- Passed checks/coverage are reused only where source and effective inputs still justify them. Repeated identical failures trigger bounded diagnosis; no arbitrary retry-count permission question or time-based quality waiver.
