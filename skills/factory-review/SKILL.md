# Factory review

Usage: [per-skill accounting](../factory-shared/gates.md#skill-token-accounting) applies to this invocation, including questions and interrupted work.

Slug: $ARGUMENTS
Session: ${CLAUDE_SESSION_ID}

The conductor runs mechanical checks and one fresh independent general review with applicable risk specialists, routes bounded validated repair batches to the existing implementation executor and records completion after current proof passes. Reviewers remain read-only; the conductor coordinates and verifies instead of writing source/test fixes or duplicating the whole-file review. A review covers one milestone ([../factory-shared/gates.md](../factory-shared/gates.md)), its slices not `done`: `M` is its number, `<last>` its last open slice, `<base>` the first slice's base commit and `<head>` the last slice's head commit. Headless milestones contain one slice. `<tmp>` is a new review temp directory, removed at hand-off. Follow this skill directly; no review step wrapper is mandatory.

## Fast lane

Track standard, interactive mode ([../factory-shared/gates.md](../factory-shared/gates.md), Milestones and autopilot). It replaces steps 1 to 6 below, which it cites.

1. **Load** as step 1, without Development proof inputs and repair checks. A `slices/<last>/review.md` with a filled Completion under approved scope and applicable passing mechanical/review/acceptance evidence means review is finished: continue directly to `/factory-reconcile <slug>`. Otherwise resume only the unresolved checks/findings, retaining earlier reports and commits. The review covers all slices: `<base>` is slice 01's base, `<head>` is HEAD. New plans authorize the repair loop in factory-plan step 6; an older explicit one-batch limit or manual pause is not silently widened without actual session authorization.
2. **Phase 1** runs the ADR enforcement prerequisite below and checks 5 to 9 once over the whole diff, before dispatching reviewers. Use the prerequisite's matching command evidence in those checks to avoid duplicate runs. Checks 1 to 4 reuse build's `checks.md` as it is, without an Inputs line; ADR-named checks still require current evidence.
3. Committed mode: commit the planning state as Phase 2 step 1 does. **Start the dev server** once, for the `runtime` scan, the UI/UX reviewer and acceptance.
4. **In parallel, once:** the general reviewer ([review-agents.md](review-agents.md)); the UI/UX and Security reviewers on Phase 2 step 2's triggers and with its prompt, the Security reviewer also whenever the diff touches sign-in, sessions or who-may-see rules; and the acceptance agent with [../factory-reconcile/SKILL.md](../factory-reconcile/SKILL.md) step 2's procedure, writing `<change>/acceptance.md` with `Tried at`. No APoSD reviewer and no Money reviewer (money is track critical). Acceptance changes data, injects failures and runs the fixed browser capability, which takes one run at a time: the `runtime` scan and the UI/UX reviewer's rendering finish on the shared server before acceptance starts, while the general and Security reviewers run beside it.
5. **One batch per repair round.** Validate and deduplicate the findings and the failed acceptance lines, as Phase 2 steps 4 to 6 say, into one batch for one executor in the checkout (build's last executor when it worked there, otherwise a fresh one), dispatched and proven as steps 7 and 8 say. Keep approved architecture, contracts and threat-model bytes unchanged.
6. **Re-check affected proof:** rerun Phase 1 checks 2 to 5 at the fix HEAD, as step 9 says, and only the reviewers whose findings were fixed, scoped to the fix diff; new risks broaden coverage. When the fix changed source, rerun the whole acceptance set so `Tried at` matches the final HEAD and reconcile can reuse it. Within actual approved scope, return remaining implementation defects to step 5 automatically until required proof passes or a real blocker remains. Repeated identical failures require a bounded diagnosis and a changed recovery, not endless identical retries. Hold only actual requirement/scope/waiver/security-outcome decisions for the user; an arbitrary repair count is not such a decision. Non-blocking residuals go to Follow-ups.
7. **Write** `slices/<last>/review.md` from step 4's template without its `Inputs` and `Owned spans` lines. Record the fix commits as one repair span checked against the union of the packets, set `<last>`'s `head_commit` to the final HEAD, and fill Completion under approved scope. Slices stay `green`. Set `notes_for_fresh_session` to "Review finished. Next: /factory-reconcile <slug>.", delete `<tmp>` and continue to reconcile.

## Track critical and small, and headless mode

### 1. Load the milestone

- For a derived correction, run gates.md's `repair-check --checkpoint edit` on load/resume before any selection or shortcut and immediately before review-fix dispatch or source edits; use its fixed start and strict exact paths, not mutable packets as scope authority.
- Load the change as gates.md describes and apply **Development proof inputs** before selecting or accepting green slices: the work is the first milestone with a slice not `done`, and every one of its slices that is not `done` must be `green`. Otherwise point to `/factory-build <slug>` and stop.
- Read each of its slices' `packet.md` and `checks.md`.
- Review only committed source. Require `git diff --quiet HEAD -- . ':!.software-factory' ':!.factory'` and `git diff --cached --quiet HEAD -- . ':!.software-factory' ':!.factory'` to succeed, and `git ls-files --others --exclude-standard -- . ':!.software-factory' ':!.factory'` to print nothing. Dirty source: inspect provenance and retain all edits. Resume the existing executor to continue and commit only known slice-owned interrupted work by exact path; unrelated dirty/staged/untracked source is a bounded blocker. Never restore or delete it to make review proceed. Never review around uncommitted source.
- When `slices/<last>/review.md` exists with an empty Sign-off and its Range ends at `<last>`'s `head_commit`, resume at step 5 only when its recorded Inputs manifest matches the current packets, genuinely approved gate hashes and effective criteria/budgets/signatures/rules, and both `git diff --quiet <head> -- . ':!.software-factory' ':!.factory'` and `git diff --cached --quiet <head> -- . ':!.software-factory' ':!.factory'` succeed. Planning-only commits may pass only when those proof inputs are unchanged. Missing historical input receipts require fresh verification/review; unchanged source is insufficient. Otherwise set `<head>` and `<last>`'s `head_commit` to the current committed HEAD and rerun from step 2, replacing the old review and its Range before completion.
- **Overlapped review**: while a `review_overlapped` event for this milestone is open (gates.md, Milestones and autopilot), the next milestone builds in the live checkout, and `<head>` is the commit that event names.
  - Make a verification clone of `<head>` in a new temp directory outside the repository, so the next milestone's test and lint runs never see it: `git -c core.hooksPath=/dev/null clone -q --no-hardlinks --no-checkout <repo> <dir>`, remove `origin`, `git checkout -q --detach <head>`. Link the live checkout's installed dependency folders into it (for example `node_modules`, `.venv`), and record in `review.md` that dependencies came from the live checkout. Phase 1, the reviewers and the clean-source check run there, and `HEAD` in sections 2 and 3 means `<head>`. The resume rule above and step 9 keep `<head>` and `<last>`'s `head_commit`: later milestones' commits never reset them.
  - The `runtime` scan (check 6) and the UI/UX reviewer need the running app: they wait for a quiet point and run against the live checkout.
  - Steps 6 to 9 and every commit in the live checkout, Phase 2 step 1's included, wait for a quiet point (gates.md), so no commit can take the building executor's staged files. Record the fix commits as a repair span of the milestone's slices, start: HEAD at the quiet point, end: the last fix commit, and leave `<last>`'s `head_commit` at `<head>`. Step 9's impacted checks include every `green` slice of the next milestone, at the new HEAD.
  - Remove the clone at hand-off.

### 2. Phase 1: mechanical checks

Build ran checks 1 to 5 on each slice and wrote them to `slices/NN/checks.md`; reuse results of checks 1 to 4 only with a matching current Inputs manifest; otherwise rerun them. Historical files without recorded inputs are not current proof. Run checks 5 to 9 once over the milestone, since a later slice can change what an earlier one's results mean. "The adapter" is `<kit>/adapters/<language>.md` for the module's `language`. Below, for one slice, `<b>` and `<test>` are its own `base_commit` and `test_commit` and "the packet" is its packet; over the milestone, `<b>` is `<base>` and the packet is the union of the milestone's packets. After a review fix commit `<fix>`, rerun impacted checks; reuse evidence only with unchanged source/test/command/dependency/environment and recorded approved/effective proof inputs with its original revision. New risks expand coverage. Require the complete mechanical suite applicable to the final source, with reused receipts explicitly identified. Red tests unchanged for the repair diffs only that commit: `git diff <fix>~1 <fix> -- <every packet's Tests first files>`. The `runtime` scan reruns only when the change touched files with that pack's `extensions`.

1. **Allowed files.** Per slice, inspect each recorded original owned span and exact repair span separately (the initial unrepaired span is its base through its own head); later slices between base and current HEAD are not attributed to it. Over the milestone, check complete history against the union of all milestone packets; a repair span recorded for an earlier milestone's overlapped review is checked against that milestone's packets instead, and checks 6 to 9 and the reviewers treat its changes as context: a finding on them is a follow-up. For each applicable span, `git diff --name-only <span start>..<span end> -- . ':!.software-factory' ':!.factory'` is a subset of the packet's Allowed files. Ordinary slices require each extra file in `deviations`; an unlogged extra is a finding. Derived corrections additionally validate every committed/staged/dirty/untracked path through `repair-check`, including reverted edits and merge parents. Their exact original-approved subset cannot expand through deviations; a wider path needs genuine scope approval.
2. **Frozen contract.** Find each signature from the packet's Frozen contract in the code. Formatting may differ. A change to the name, parameters, types, return type, errors or visibility is a difference.
3. **Red tests unchanged.** Compare test changes in the original slice-owned GREEN span after `<test>` and its exact repair spans; inspect each change against the current Tests first expectation and logged correction/proof. A change is unchanged or has a valid recorded deviation and supplementary proof. Later slices' separately authorized test edits are checked in their own spans, not mislabelled as this slice's deviation. Inspect current tests against refreshed criteria as well; span attribution cannot excuse semantic drift.
4. **Green.** `test_command` passes at HEAD, apart from tests in `baseline_failures`.
5. **Lint.** `check_command`; each failure in the changed files is a finding. No `check_command`: skipped.
6. **Language checks.** For each module whose `<kit>/languages/<pack>.yaml` has `checks`, `{files}` is the changed files with the listed `extensions`: `git diff --name-only --diff-filter=d <b>..HEAD`, made relative to the module path. No such files: skip the module. Otherwise, from the module directory:
   - `static` on `{files}`. Each issue it reports is a finding.
   - `runtime`, on tracks standard and critical, when the module has a `dev_url` and the tool resolves (`npx --no-install <tool> --version`). If `dev_url` already answers, use that server and leave it running. Otherwise start the module's `dev_command` in the background, wait up to 60 seconds for `dev_url`, run the scan, and stop the server you started on every outcome. Each issue in the JSON is a finding.
   - A tool that is not installed, a server that never answers, or a scan that cannot start (for example, no browser) is reported as skipped, with the error or the `install` line.
7. **Dependency rule.** When the module's approved `Pattern` line selected archgraph, use its pinned rule run from check 9 instead of the adapter's import-graph listing; reuse that result, with new dependency violations tagged `[clean-architecture/dependency rule]`. Otherwise, for each module with `layers` in `lenses.yaml` (globs relative to the module path, where `{a,b}` matches either), list the imports of the changed files with the Import graph command of the adapter for each file's language. An import the diff adds from a file in one layer into a later (outer) layer is a finding tagged `[clean-architecture/dependency rule]`; one the file already had at `<b>` is not. Files in no layer are not checked.
8. **Dead code.** The adapter's Dead code command; each result in the changed files is a finding, except frozen signatures, step definitions a scenario uses, and code a constraint's enforcement names. A tool that is not installed is reported as skipped.
9. **Layout.** Each file the diff adds (`git diff --name-only --no-renames --diff-filter=A <b>..HEAD -- . ':!.software-factory' ':!.factory'`) fits the Layout (gates.md, Layout). Each one that does not is a finding. For each `Pattern` line in `02-architecture.md` or `.software-factory/system/layout.md` whose `check` is not `none`, also run that tool's `command` from `<kit>/layouts/tools.yaml`, from its root. Each issue on a line the diff adds or changes, or about a file or folder the diff adds, is a finding; a tool that is not installed is reported as skipped with its `install` line.

**ADR enforcement prerequisite.** Before any Phase 2 reviewer, follow [../factory-shared/adr.md](../factory-shared/adr.md): inventory accepted ADRs at the reviewed HEAD, then run `python3 <kit>/adr.py check --repo <repo> --checks <tmp>/adr-checks.json --output <tmp>/adr-checks-result.json` with the documented manifest. Run this prerequisite first: its named tests supply check 4, lint supplies check 5 and architecture rules supply check 9 when command, source and configuration match. The helper deduplicates identical commands within this invocation; it does not import historical green receipts. Run each accepted ADR's named enforcement even when its files are outside the diff. Record each failure as `[adr/NNNN]` in Phase 1 and use the existing repair/hold route before spending a model call. A missing named rule/test, timeout, unavailable tool or unverified monitor is a proof gap, never a pass; report the reason and install line where applicable. ADRs without an enforcement line are human-verified for compatibility. Retain the inventory and result hashes with review evidence; changes to an ADR or its enforcement invalidate reuse.

Give reviewers the accepted ADR folder path and the relevant inventory entries: human-verified ADRs whose Context intersects the change, and behavioral ADRs whose named tests/rules the diff touches, including green tests whose meaning may have changed. A green check establishes its tested outcome, not every implication of the Decision. Do not ask a model to repeat a failed mechanical check.

A frozen-contract difference sends its slice back, as gates.md describes.

### 3. Phase 2: review agents and lens reviewers

1. Committed mode: `git add .software-factory/changes/<slug> .software-factory/system` and, when anything is staged, commit `chore(factory): milestone M review`, so the review starts from a clean tree. In interactive mode this commit also carries the planning state since the last one (gates.md, Commits).
2. Start the applicable risk reviewers in the background, one fresh sub-agent each, each lens checked as [../factory-shared/packs.md](../factory-shared/packs.md) describes and read from its resolved pack folder:
   - **Optional design books** are carried by the existing general reviewer only when explicitly selected in this task's `design_lenses`, within packs.md's size/excerpt cap. APoSD does not get an automatic extra agent for a new public symbol; contract conformance remains mandatory.
   - **UI/UX**, when the diff changes rendered behavior or accessibility (including UI entry points); uncertainty triggers this reviewer: a sub-agent follows the `lens` mode of [../factory-ui-ux/SKILL.md](../factory-ui-ux/SKILL.md) for the routes the milestone touches, and writes its findings as cards anchored to the design file or WCAG.
   - **Security**, when the diff changes trust-boundary behavior, authentication/authorization, secrets, input validation or security-sensitive entry points; use the threat model when present, but an absent/stale model never proves no risk. Uncertain risk triggers this reviewer: a sub-agent follows the `lens` mode of [../factory-security/SKILL.md](../factory-security/SKILL.md) on the milestone diff, and writes its confirmed findings as cards.
   - **Money**, when financial arithmetic, idempotency or financial outcomes are affected; retain critical money coverage and trigger this reviewer when risk is uncertain: `fin-verification/SKILL.md` with the one domain skill that fits the milestone (`fin-ledger`, `fin-payments`, `fin-onchain` or `fin-exchange-integration`), or with `fin-money-core` when none fits. `fin-money-core` joins a domain skill only when the risk is arithmetic or idempotency itself. Each pack is read through `SKILL.md` in its cache folder.

   Each receives:

   > Remain read-only: report findings, never edit source/tests. Review `git diff <base>..HEAD -- . ':!.software-factory' ':!.factory'` in `<repo>` through `<lens files>`. The agreed contracts are `<packet paths>`; judge what the code does behind them. Write at most three findings to `<tmp>/<lens>.md`, each as a challenge card (`<absolute path of factory-interface/challenge-card.md>`) read for code: Where quotes the changed line, Alternative is the code change, and Response will be fix or skip. Severity `block` or `fix` needs an anchor: a constraint ID in a packet's Covers, an accepted ADR in `.software-factory/system/adr/` cited by number whose Decision the changed line contradicts (tag `[adr/NNNN]`), the interface comment of a frozen signature the code contradicts, a threat ID in `.software-factory/system/threat-model.md` or in the `## Threats` section of `02-architecture.md`, a word in the `## Glossary` Never column of `01-product.md`, a Voice line of the design file, a principle, position or word of the design file, a WCAG 2.2 A or AA criterion, or a verified applicable security/money rule with concrete reproduction; every other finding is a `note`. A finding about a rendered screen quotes the changed line that renders the element.

3. Run the review agents through their report, as [review-agents.md](review-agents.md) describes, with these factory rules on top:
   - the packets' Covers and Frozen contracts are agreed and are not design findings
   - `check_command` failures are fixed only inside the Allowed files; failures elsewhere are findings
   - out-of-diff findings are recorded as follow-ups in `review.md`, not fixed in this milestone
   - findings from a rule under the merged Quiet rules of the principle layers ([../factory-shared/gates.md](../factory-shared/gates.md), Paths), money and security rules excepted, are recommended as skip
   - rubric for the agents, each finding from a rubric tagged `[lens/rule]`, each rubric pack checked as packs.md describes and read from its resolved pack folder:
     - design and reuse: the concise project Principles and simplification ladder, plus only explicitly selected task-sized excerpts. No default Refactoring.Guru, APoSD, Clean Architecture or pattern-map load. When an explicitly selected excerpt depends on a new public symbol, use archgraph's public-surface result when available, with [the documented fallback](../factory-shared/archgraph.md) otherwise; this never activates a lens or adds an agent. Basic reuse, excessive complexity, side-effect correctness and mandatory mechanical boundary checks do not need a book.
     - design and efficiency: the module's language pack index; only its CRITICAL and HIGH categories are findings, the rest are notes
     - cleanliness: each test under a packet's Tests first asserts the outcome its line names, and for an FR the outcome of its Example in Covers; a test that only calls the code, or only checks that it does not throw, is a finding tagged `[factory/example-asserted]`
     - cleanliness, in modules with `bdd: native`: duplicate steps, steps that name clicks, fields or URLs, and wording that differs from the product's rules

   Give the independent general reviewer one bounded missing-outcome pass over the milestone’s user-visible behavior: at most three reproducible violations of the product Problem, Success metric, agreed persistence decisions or standing promises. Each records the goal/promise quoted, concrete initial state and event sequence, expected and actual user outcome, changed code location, and reproduction evidence. Do not invent a requirement ID. Data loss or a false “saved” message is not downgraded merely because its precise scenario is absent from Covers. Record these separately as `[factory/missing-outcome]` held findings; a scenario already covered by an ID remains an ordinary conformance finding. Within-contract fixes follow the existing Allowed files and regression-test rules. An omitted promise or changed frozen contract is proposed for correction at sign-off and uses the existing send-back path after the user decides; never rewrite approved contracts or Tests first silently.

4. Interactive mode: select every validated recommended finding inside Allowed files and frozen contracts that retains Tests first semantics for one executor batch; do not apply it yet. A wrong executable test under an unchanged promise follows the build deviation and isolated-proof rule. Others are skipped or held for the actual scope/contract decision; the user sees all at sign-off. Then go on to step 5.

   Headless mode: the report is the question. Write its findings and the lens cards to `slices/<last>/proposed.md`, then wait for the user (gates.md), asking for the numbers to fix (`fix 1 3`, or `fix none`). The run that receives the reply validates those fixes from `proposed.md` and routes the bounded batch to the existing executor as review-agents.md's repair-batch step describes, continues at step 6, and deletes `proposed.md` once `review.md` is written.
5. Observe every reviewer’s native terminal completion, then read its exact report path. A written file or progress message alone is not completion. Collect the lens cards. Drop a card whose quoted line is not in the diff; a `block` or `fix` card whose anchor does not exist, or whose lens and rule are under Quiet rules of the principle layers ([../factory-shared/gates.md](../factory-shared/gates.md), Paths), money and security rules excepted, becomes a `note`. Interactive mode: merge every remaining `block` and `fix` card into the same validated executor batch, without asking, and skip notes. A card is held for the user at sign-off instead when its Alternative changes a frozen signature (a possible send-back), touches a file outside the Allowed files, or changes a Tests first expectation. Executable-test corrections under an unchanged promise are local repairs with deviations and isolated proof. Include each card only if its quoted line still reads the same in the working tree; a card whose line changed is reported in `review.md` as stale.
6. A derived correction uses its existing approved test paths only: no new regression file or typecheck deviation may expand `repair.json` paths. A required extra path follows the genuine slice approval route. For ordinary slices, a fix may add its regression test in a new test file beside the slice's tests: before writing it, log `{ slice, file, reason: "regression test for finding N" }` in `deviations` and append the file to that slice's packet's Allowed files. Any other fix the factory hook blocks (a file outside the Allowed files) is not applied: it becomes a follow-up.
7. Dispatch the one deduplicated validated batch to the existing implementation executor (fresh only if unavailable or unsafe to resume), after derived-correction edit validation when applicable. Pass exact findings/anchors/reproductions, expected unchanged criteria/contracts, allowed paths, packet/input references and affected verification commands; preserve the full frozen contract. The executor makes minimal fixes, stages only authorized paths and commits `fix(<slug>): milestone M review`, retaining exact correction spans and test deviations. Observe its actual native terminal completion and validate returned commits/artifacts; a file or progress message is not completion. Preserve unrelated dirty work and block unattended completion on it. Neither reviewers nor conductor make routine source/test fixes.
8. Each added or corrected regression test proves itself in an isolated checkout: `python3 <kit>/runtime.py red --repo <absolute repo> --commit <fix parent> --tests-from <fix commit> --test-file <exact test path> --checkpoint <absolute slice checkpoint> --timeout <budget> -- <test command>`. Repeat `--test-file` for each test. Read assertion failures, retain source/test commits, hashes and logs, and run the identical tests at current HEAD for GREEN. Do not restore live production files. Fixed browser requests cannot overlay tests; require a valid recorded pre-fix source/test commit through that capability or record a bounded blocker. Setup failure, timeout or an already-green test is not regression proof; report the precise limit, retaining valid coverage.
9. Rerun impacted Phase 1 checks and obtain one scoped independent confirmation of each fix batch and any test correction/exemption. Fast lane: re-check only with the reviewers whose findings were fixed, on that fix diff. New risks broaden coverage; unaffected review is reused at its recorded revision. Require final applicable mechanical proof before updating `<last>`'s `head_commit`. A broken fix returns as a bounded evidence batch to the same executor, retaining history; do not discard concurrent work or treat a retry count as an approval question.

### 4. Write `slices/<last>/review.md`

```markdown
# Review: milestone M, slices NN to <last>

Range: <base>..<head>
Inputs: <per-slice packet/check input manifests, exact approved gate hashes and effective proof inputs>
Owned spans: <original per-slice spans and exact repair spans, with original heads/repair-start HEADs retained>

## Phase 1
- Per slice: `slices/NN/checks.md`, each pass | <its failures>
- Milestone checks 5 to 9: pass | <findings> | skipped: <reason>
- After review fixes: pass | <failures> | no fixes
- Red: each slice's test_commit failed on its new tests. Green: <head> passes test_command.

## ADR enforcement
<accepted ADR inventory revision/hash, named checks and matching receipts; each failure tagged `[adr/NNNN]`, and each skipped/unverified enforcement with its reason; no accepted ADRs: none>

## Review agents
<one line per finding, numbered as the report numbered it: `N. [<tag>] <severity> <path>: <finding> -> <outcome>`, where the severity follows the report's recommendation: a finding it recommends fixing is `fix`, one it recommends skipping is `note`, and `block` comes only from lens cards, the outcome one of: `fix` or `skip` (the user's answer), `auto-fixed` (applied without asking), `auto-skipped` (left out of step 4's fixes), `undone` (auto-fixed, then undone by the user), `follow-up` or `dropped`. The tag is the rubric rule, written as interface cards write Lens / rule, or, without one, `review/<category>` (correctness, cleanliness, design, efficiency, gating). Keep `[adr/NNNN]` on ADR-anchored cards and mechanical findings, alongside a rubric tag when useful; citing an ADR adds no severity. Preserve each finding's anchor, reproduction/proof and repair or held-decision reference for retro attribution. Findings dropped after validation get their own lines. Last, the report's coverage line.>

## Lens reviewers
<each card, with its outcome: `fix` or `skip` (the user's answer, a held card included), `auto-fixed`, `auto-skipped` (a note), `undone`, or `stale`; "not run" with the reason when a reviewer did not apply>

## Missing outcomes
<at most three held `[factory/missing-outcome]` findings with goal/promise, reproduction, evidence and proposed contract correction; none when no violation reproduced>

## Follow-ups
<out-of-diff findings>

## Completion under approved scope
<actual initial approval reference, unchanged scope, independent review revision and mechanical proof; empty until verified>

## Sign-off
<final actual human name and ISO time; empty until supplied>
```

### 5. Complete or hold the milestone

For a derived correction, run gates.md's `repair-check --checkpoint complete` with its current recorded head before any `done` write. Failure blocks completion without changing parents or approved documents. Scope/provenance success never supplies independent review, native terminal completion, RED/GREEN or acceptance proof.

Interactive mode: when actual initial execution approval covers the unchanged scope, independent review covers current source and current approved/effective proof inputs, and all required mechanical evidence passes, fill `Completion under approved scope` and set the milestone slices to `done`. Leave `Sign-off` empty. Record auto-fixes and held decisions; proceed to the next milestone without repeated approval. Unknown authority or missing proof blocks completion. A change to promised behavior, a frozen signature, security/money outcome or required coverage uses the appropriate real decision/send-back path. Independent held notes can wait for the consolidated reconcile question; unresolved blockers cannot be disguised as completion.

If the user requested manual milestone sign-off, or existing session approval does not cover automatic completion, show the diff/review and obtain the actual decision. Preserve any requested undo through a scoped commit and fresh affected checks/review. Never infer consent from status or a file hash.

Headless mode: commit and push `review.md`, then wait for the user (gates.md) with the comment "Slice NN reviewed: `review.md`. Sign off with a pull-request review whose first line is `sign off slice NN`." The runner records the sign-off.

### 6. Hand off

Interactive mode only: a headless run stops at step 5's wait, and the runner records the sign-off.

1. Delete `<tmp>`. Nothing is committed here (gates.md, Commits).
2. Set `notes_for_fresh_session` to "Milestone M done. Next: /factory-build <slug>." or, when all slices are done, "All slices done. Next: /factory-reconcile <slug>.", and continue directly under approved scope; with `--one-step` or a manual pause, tell the user the next skill and stop.
