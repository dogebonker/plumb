# Source-applicability probe

Both raw and curated inputs matched the six predeclared scenario decisions.
All six bundle answers and five raw answers passed citation validation. The
remaining raw answer reached the expected unknown/missing-evidence decision,
but its source quote was absent or ambiguous in the bound original.

The cases distinguished fixed code, old code, explicitly unresolved competing
claims, an explicit correction, a future proposal and absent implementation.
The unresolved-authority and proposal-role presentations were controlled test
inputs, not claims about actual Pond approval history.

Provider list-cost observations were $0.576135 for raw documents and $0.101100
for the selected verbatim excerpts. This is one pass with much smaller selected
context, not a measured repeatable speedup or net curation benefit. Human effort
remains unmeasured. Selection preserved the source wording; no new requirement
was paraphrased into the bundle.

This result motivated the subsequent [end-to-end hill-climb](../hillclimb/RESULTS.md),
which tests actual correction/application rather than only scenario decisions.
Original [protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [label review](LABEL_REVIEW.md) and
[observations](runs/summary.json) remain intact.
